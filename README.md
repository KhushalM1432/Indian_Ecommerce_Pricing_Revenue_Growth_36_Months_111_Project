# Indian_Ecommerce_Pricing_Revenue_Growth_36_Months_111_Project
# Data Science
## 🔹 Project Highlights
1. Analyzed **36 months of Indian e-commerce sales data** to identify key factors influencing pricing, discounts, and revenue growth.
2. Performed **end-to-end data preprocessing**, including handling missing values, duplicates, data type validation, and dataset preparation for analysis.
3. Conducted **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to uncover trends in revenue, sales performance, product categories, brands, and customer purchasing behavior.
4. Evaluated the impact of **pricing strategies, discount percentages, and promotional sales events** on overall business revenue and sales performance.
5. Performed **customer segmentation and regional analysis** by age group, gender, state, and business zone to identify high-performing markets.
6. Analyzed **competition intensity and inventory pressure** to understand their influence on revenue and support business decision-making.
7. Developed insightful **Matplotlib and Seaborn visualizations** and generated actionable business recommendations for pricing optimization, revenue growth, and strategic planning.
